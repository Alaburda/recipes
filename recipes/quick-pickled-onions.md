## Ingredients

- 2 red onions, very thinly sliced
- 300 ml pickle brine
- 1 tsp coriander seeds
- 1 dried chilli

## Method

1. Slice the onions as thinly as you can manage — a mandolin earns its keep here.
2. Pack them into a clean jar with the coriander seeds and the chilli. Push them down; they compress a lot.
3. Pour over the brine until the onions are covered. Seal.
4. Ready in an hour, properly pink and soft by the next day. Keeps two weeks in the fridge.
