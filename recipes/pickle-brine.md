---
title: "All-Purpose Pickle Brine"
description: One jar of brine, endless quick pickles. Keeps for a month.
categories: [base, vegan, make-ahead, no-cook]
time: 10 min
servings: 500 ml
---

## Ingredients

- 250 ml white wine vinegar
- 250 ml water
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 1 tbsp salt
- 1 tsp black peppercorns
- 2 bay leaves

## Method

1. Put everything in a small pan and warm gently, stirring, just until the sugar and salt dissolve. Do not boil it — you lose the sharpness.
2. Take it off the heat and let it cool to room temperature.
3. Pour into a clean jar. It keeps in the fridge for a month.

Ratios matter more than the exact ingredients: equal vinegar and water, then salt and sugar to taste.
